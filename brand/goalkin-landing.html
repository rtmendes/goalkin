Circles and clubs

One shared goal. Everyone's own reasons.

A club, a small group or a family shares one goal and one progress bar. Each member adds their own layer underneath: a fundraiser they want the world to see, a number they keep to themselves, a habit only the group sees.

Leaders invite by link, schedule meetups onto everyone's calendar, and see who might need a nudge.

Focus rooms

Show up together.

Open a focus room, invite your kin, and work in 25-minute rounds on a shared timer. Everyone says what they're working on at the start and checks in at the end, so the session counts toward each person's goal.
