## What has to be true
- ≥ 1 in 4 program buyers upgrades to Pro within 30 days
- Pro monthly churn ≤ 8%
- Cost per new paying buyer ≤ $30 (the $37 programs pay it back on the first sale)
- ~1 template per Pro member every 3–4 months
- 50+ active creators by month six averaging $3,000/month in sales
